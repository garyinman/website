My approach

ESG as practical change that sticks.

Most ESG advice is written for big corporates and ends up in a policy folder. For a growing business, it only works if it becomes part of how the business runs.

Part of the business

Built into everyday work, from the office to the site, so it improves how you operate as well as how you report.

Carried by your people

Change lasts when the people doing the work understand it and own it. That's where my background in people and culture makes the difference.

Joined up with everything else

ESG connects to your bids, quality systems, culture and reputation. I keep the whole picture in view, so each piece strengthens the others.

Proportionate and achievable

Start with what you already do, keep it simple to run, and build from there at a pace the business can sustain.

It's grounded in real experience. I'm Certified Carbon Literate, and in a growing London construction business I helped introduce social value from scratch, now rated Very Good by NetPositive, and develop a simple carbon tracking model the team actually uses.

ESG in bids

A genuine strength in tenders.

Large contracts often come with formal ESG requirements. On smaller ones, under £5m, buyers still ask, and expect a sensible, proportionate answer.

Many SMEs answer ESG and social value questions with generic statements. Clear, honest, evidenced answers stand out straight away.

  • Answers grounded in what you actually do, with real examples
  • Commitments sized to the contract, that you can deliver and report on
  • Evidence ready to reuse from one tender to the next
  • Gaps spotted early, with a practical plan to close them
