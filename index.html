Construction

I know how construction works.

Leading people with very different working lives, under constant commercial pressure.

Big on site, lean at the centre.

Construction businesses often run for years with central teams far smaller than their turnover and site footprint would suggest. People, ESG, bids, systems and reporting rest on a few shoulders, until growth, a bigger contract or a new requirement exposes the gap.

Site and office

Two cultures, often pulling different ways. Change has to work in both.

A mixed workforce

Direct staff, subcontractors and labour-only teams, many first languages, apprentices to forty years on the tools.

Relationships carry the business

Clients, main contractors, supply chain and long-serving people. Growth strains all of them.

Founder-led, or growing beyond the founders

What made the business succeed often lives in a few people. Growth means sharing it without losing it.

Keeping pace with what’s changing
  • The Building Safety Act’s focus on competence and accountability, and what it asks of people
  • The Construction Leadership Council’s Mental Health Joint Code of Practice
  • Social value and carbon in tenders
  • Practical, safe use of AI
